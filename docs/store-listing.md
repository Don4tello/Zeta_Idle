## Full description  (max 4000 chars)

```
⚔️ ZETA IDLE — Choose Your Class. Conquer the Darkness.

A dark medieval idle RPG that pairs the depth of a D&D-inspired build system with the relaxed pace of an idle game. Forge a hero, auto-battle through a cursed realm, and keep growing — even while you're away.

Your hero fights, loots, and earns gold automatically, including offline. Drop in for a few minutes to spend upgrades and refine your build, or sit back and watch the power climb.

⚜️ BUILD YOUR HERO
• 12 classes — Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
• 6 races, each with unique traits that reshape your playstyle
• Six damage types — Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Poison, Void
• A deep active-ability system with upgrade milestones and devastating ultimates

🗡️ MASTER THE COMBAT
• A 100-stage handcrafted CAMPAIGN culminating in the dreaded Omega Absolute
• DUNGEON — a roguelite run with branching paths, traps, shrines, and item drops
• BOSS RUSH, GAUNTLET modifiers, and the endless TOWER ASCENSION
• PvP ARENA — climb the ranked ladder against other heroes

🛡️ ENDLESS PROGRESSION
• A branching passive skill tree and permanent ability scores
• Forge, upgrade, and craft equipment; socket gems and runes
• Collectible ARTIFACTS with powerful 2- and 3-piece set bonuses
• Elemental Mastery to specialise your damage type
• PRESTIGE / REBIRTH for Paragon points, run-shaping boons, and optional challenge runs

🤝 COMPANIONS & GUILDS
• Recruit NPC mercenaries with battle-changing abilities
• Collect and evolve loyal pets
• Join a GUILD — chat, raid weekly bosses, and wage territory wars

🎯 ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
Daily challenges, quests, bounties, expeditions, achievements, a monster bestiary, and cosmetic skins & auras to make your hero your own.

Zeta Idle is free to play with optional purchases. Ads are never forced — watch a rewarded ad only if you choose to.

Choose your class. Conquer the darkness. Begin your ascension today.
```
