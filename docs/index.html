An Idle RPG Built for Depth

Zeta Idle blends the accessibility of idle games with the strategic depth of tabletop RPGs. Whether you're grinding offline or hand-crafting your passive skill tree, every decision shapes your legend.

"You step onto ancient burial grounds. The dead here do not rest — they march."

Battle through 20 atmospheric zones, each with unique enemies and named bosses. Prestige to unlock permanent upgrades and discover ever-deeper layers of progression — from artifact forges to class mastery questlines.

Core Features

A layered idle RPG experience — accessible to start, endlessly deep to master.

⚔

D&D-Style Combat

Turn-based battles with a full damage pipeline: proficiency scaling, armor class, 13 status effects, elemental damage types, and critical hits inspired by tabletop dice.

∞

Idle & Auto-Battle

Your hero fights even while you're away. Gold accumulates every 5 seconds, the campaign runs in the background, and expeditions collect resources on 30-minute to 8-hour timers.

△

Deep Ability System

Each class unlocks 6 active abilities and an ultimate from levels 1–30. Rank abilities up to 165, choosing a milestone path at every 5 ranks for hundreds of build combinations.

◆

Prestige & Ascension

Rebirth at stage milestones to earn Souls for permanent upgrades. Progress through Prestige tiers, unlock the Ascension Tower, and invest in Legacy Power for compounding bonuses.

☯

Passive Skill Tree

Six branches — Slayer, Guardian, Merchant, Mystic, Elementalist, Ascendant — with cross-branch connectors and Keystones like Bloodlust, Primordial Core, and Convergence Prime.

⛭

Equipment & Artifacts

11 equipment slots, 8 rarity tiers up to Unique, set bonuses, item keywords like Life Steal and Void Step, plus an Artifact Forge for crafting legendary relics from Mythril.

Game Modes

From idle auto-battle to competitive PvP — unlock new ways to play as you progress.

Always Available

Campaign

Fight through 20 zones of cursed enemies and named bosses. Auto-battle while idle or watch through a live battle log. Push to stage 43 and face The Omega.

Stage 15

Dungeon

Multi-room challenges — 3 runs per day. Find locked chests for shards and rare loot.

Stage 25

Boss Rush

Face every campaign boss back-to-back. 2 attempts per day. Earn Mythril for the Artifact Forge.

Stage 45

Gauntlet

Survival mode with escalating difficulty. 5 attempts per day. Earn Echoes for permanent upgrades.

Stage 40

Expeditions

Send mercenaries on 30-min to 8-hour resource runs. Collect gold, shards, and materials offline.

Stage 50

PvP Arena

Battle other heroes with stamina-based matchmaking. Climb the leaderboard and earn Gem Shards.

Endless Progression

Layer upon layer of systems to master — each reset makes you stronger than before.

Prestige System

Rebirth at stage milestones (25, 50, 75, 100+) to earn Souls. Spend them on permanent upgrades like Iron Resolve, Blood Drinker, and Death's Edge. Each cycle compounds exponentially.

Soul Currency Prestige Shop Prestige Titles Endless Mode

Passive Skill Tree

Six branches of passive nodes spanning combat, defense, economy, and magic. Unlock cross-branch connectors and reach powerful Keystones that reshape your entire playstyle.

6 Branches Keystones Connectors Class Nodes

Ascension Tower

Unlocked after Prestige 3 — 8 nodes including Eternal Wisdom, Gilded Path, Titan Strength, and Soul Harvest. Soul investment grants compounding bonuses across all systems.

8 Nodes Post-Prestige 3 Legacy Power

Artifact Forge

Forge powerful artifacts from Mythril earned in Boss Rush. Books, charms, gemstones, swords, and tapestries — each with unique properties and rare legendary variants.

Mythril Crafting 6 Artifact Types Rare Variants

Rune System

Post-prestige, disenchant unwanted items for Rune Dust. Craft runes to enchant equipment with powerful affixes that push builds beyond what base loot provides.

Rune Dust Disenchanting Enchanting Post-Prestige

Companions & Pets

12 class-themed pets provide passive bonuses. Hire 8 NPC allies with unique talents and pair synergies — from Ruk the Stone Golem to Cael the Warmaster.
